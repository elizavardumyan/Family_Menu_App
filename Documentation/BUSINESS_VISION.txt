
===========================================================
Family Menu App
BUSINESS VISION
Founder: Eliza Vardumyan
===========================================================

MISSION

Help families save time, reduce stress, reduce food waste,
and make everyday meal planning simple.

-----------------------------------------------------------

PROBLEM

Families spend too much time deciding:
- What to cook
- What to buy
- How much it will cost
- Whether ingredients are already at home

-----------------------------------------------------------

SOLUTION

A mobile application that allows families to:

• Plan meals
• Store recipes
• Automatically generate shopping lists
• Calculate shopping costs
• Organize products
• Support multiple languages

-----------------------------------------------------------

LONG-TERM FEATURES

✓ Meal Planner
✓ Recipe Database
✓ Shopping List
✓ Product Prices
✓ Multi-language Support
